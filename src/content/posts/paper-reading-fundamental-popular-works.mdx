## Transformer

*"Attention Is All You Need"* (Vaswani et al., 2017). The paper that introduced the self-attention architecture which underpins almost every modern sequence model.

## RT-2

*"RT-2: Vision-Language-Action Model Transfers Web Knowledge to Robotic Control"* (Brohan et al., 2023). Co-training a vision-language model with robotic data to obtain a single model that can see, understand, and act.

## I-JEPA

*"Revisiting Feature Prediction for Learning Visual Representations from Unconstrained Data"* (Assran et al., 2023). A method that predicts missing regions in latent space rather than pixels, learning representations without reconstruction.
